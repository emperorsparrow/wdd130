Sparrow Rafting Company

Purpose: The purpose of this company is to give every visitor/ tourist a peek into the wonderful water lines and the rich ecosystem of the Niger river flowing through the Southern part of Nigeria into the Atlantic ocean. Mission: Our mission is to give you an adventure unlike any other you have ever experienced as an individual, family or group. Creed: We strongly believe that we can stamp out water body pollution by exposing to you the dangers of dumping plastic and other non-biodegradable waste into the water bodies and to also help you appreciate the beauty of the rapids, water falls and shorelines of our river. Motto: One happy and satisfied customer can make all the difference.
